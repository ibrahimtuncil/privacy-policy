Our application is strictly intended for individuals who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If you are under 18, please do not use or attempt to use this Application. Our application does not directly collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. The application does not require any user registration or account creation.

These ad networks and third-party services may collect and use anonymous device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and technical device information to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements, analyze ad performance, and prevent fraud.
